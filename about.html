I believe people deserve to feel heard, informed, and supported.

Hi, I’m Loreen DeGandi, an Independent Patient Advocate with a deep passion for helping patients and families feel more prepared throughout their healthcare journey.

My path into patient advocacy grew from personal experience navigating complex medical situations alongside people I love. I learned how overwhelming healthcare can become when families are trying to understand diagnoses, medications, treatment options, medical records, care transitions, insurance systems, and difficult decisions all at the same time.

My Story

I discovered something important about myself: I love finding answers.

I am naturally drawn to research, organization, resource finding, and asking thoughtful questions. I enjoy taking complicated information and turning it into something that feels understandable and manageable.

Most importantly, I care deeply about making sure people feel listened to and included in conversations about their own care or the care of someone they love.

Advocacy has taught me that sometimes the most meaningful support is simply having another person beside you who is willing to listen carefully, help organize the pieces, research the questions you do not know how to ask yet, and remind you that your concerns deserve to be taken seriously.
My Approach

Compassionate, thorough, collaborative, and patient centered.

01

Listen carefully

Your concerns, priorities, and goals come first. Advocacy begins with understanding what matters to you.

02

Research thoughtfully

I help locate reputable information and organize it so the questions and possibilities are easier to understand.

03

Prepare clearly

Questions, timelines, medication lists, resources, and next steps become more useful when they are easy to navigate.

04

Protect your voice

You remain in control of your decisions. My role is to help you feel more prepared to participate in your care.

Where I Am Today

Building this work thoughtfully and ethically.

I am currently building hands on experience through volunteer patient advocacy while continuing my education and working toward professional patient advocacy certification.

This stage of my journey matters deeply to me. I want to build my advocacy practice with care, strong boundaries, and respect for the responsibility that comes with supporting people during vulnerable moments in their lives.

❀

What my role is

I help patients and families become more organized, informed, prepared, and confident when navigating care.

What my role is not

I do not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe treatment, replace licensed healthcare professionals, or provide legal advice.

Why This Matters to Me

Healthcare can feel frightening and confusing, especially when someone you love is sick.

No one should feel like they have to understand everything alone.

Sometimes you need someone who will sit beside you, look through the information with you, help you find the right questions, and keep searching when the path forward is unclear.

That is the kind of advocate I hope to be.
