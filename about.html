I believe people deserve to feel heard, informed, and supported.

Hi, I’m Loreen DeGandi, an Independent Patient Advocate with a deep interest in medical research, healthcare navigation, careful documentation, and helping patients and families prepare for difficult care conversations.

My Story

I discovered something important about myself. I love finding answers.

My path into patient advocacy grew from personal experience navigating complex medical situations alongside people I love.

I learned how quickly healthcare can become overwhelming when families are trying to understand medical information, medications, referrals, rehabilitation, care transitions, records, and difficult decisions all at the same time.

I also learned how much careful research, organized documentation, thoughtful questions, and persistent follow up can matter. Somewhere in the middle of all of that work, I realized that advocacy was not only something I cared deeply about. It was work I felt called to keep doing.

Today, I use those strengths to help other patients and caregivers feel more prepared. I am especially drawn to situations where the information is complicated, the next step feels unclear, or a family needs help turning a long list of concerns into a focused plan.

“I want the person I support to leave feeling more prepared, more informed, and more confident in their own voice.”

My Role

I support your voice. I do not replace licensed care.

My work is independent and nonclinical. I can help research, organize, prepare, locate resources, and support communication. I do not diagnose, prescribe, provide treatment, make decisions for you, or provide legal advice.
