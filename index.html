About Loreen

I’m an independent patient advocate dedicated to helping patients and families navigate healthcare with more clarity, confidence, and support. My path into advocacy began through personal experience with complex medical care and a determination to keep researching when the next step was not clear.

Support that feels human, thoughtful, and personal.

Services I Offer

Practical support for complicated healthcare moments.

Every situation is different. Support is tailored around your questions, priorities, and next steps.

Patient Navigation

Helping you make sense of complex healthcare systems, referrals, specialists, and next steps.

Medical Research

Researching conditions, treatments, rehabilitation, specialists, and care options using reliable sources.

Appointment Prep

Organizing symptoms, concerns, history, and focused questions so important details are easier to discuss.

Care Coordination

Supporting clearer communication between patients, caregivers, providers, facilities, and services.

Resource Connection

Finding community, financial, disability, caregiver, transportation, and other support resources.

Record Organization

Creating clearer timelines, medication lists, care summaries, question sheets, and records checklists.

Patient Rights & Support

Helping patients understand their options, clarify priorities, and strengthen their voice in care conversations.

Caregiver Support

Offering a calm place to organize concerns, prepare for conversations, and work through possible next steps.

Who I Help

Support for people carrying a lot at once.

  • Patients and families facing complex medical situations
  • Caregivers who need help organizing information and next steps
  • People preparing for important appointments or care conferences
  • Families navigating neurological injury, rehabilitation, or long term care
  • Patients trying to locate specialists, resources, or additional support
Why This Matters

Healthcare can feel overwhelming. Advocacy can make the path clearer.

I know what it feels like to have a family medical situation become a maze of records, medications, unanswered questions, specialists, and difficult decisions. My goal is not to take over your voice. It is to help you feel more prepared to use it.

I’m here to help you find the questions, information, and next steps that matter. ♡
