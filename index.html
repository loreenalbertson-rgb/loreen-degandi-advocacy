About Me

Advocacy rooted in compassion, curiosity, and persistence.

I know how quickly medical information can become confusing, intimidating, and emotionally exhausting. My role is to help make the process feel more manageable.

I bring a research-focused mindset, careful organization, and a strong commitment to making sure patients and caregivers have space to ask questions, understand their options, and communicate what matters most to them.

My goal is simple: help you feel less alone and more prepared for whatever comes next.

My Approach

Gentle support. Thoughtful research. Clear next steps.

Advocacy should never make you feel talked over. I work collaboratively, helping you identify what matters, what questions remain, and what information may help you move forward.

1

3

A Clear Boundary

Advocacy supports your care. It does not replace your care team.

I provide independent, nonclinical support focused on research, organization, preparation, communication, and navigation. Medical decisions should always be made with qualified healthcare professionals. For emergencies, contact emergency services or your treating medical team.
