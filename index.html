About Loreen

Support rooted in compassion, curiosity, and persistence.

Read my story →

My path into patient advocacy began through personal experience with complex medical care and the determination to keep searching when the next step was not clear.

I am naturally drawn to research, organization, resource finding, careful documentation, and thoughtful questions. My goal is to help patients and caregivers feel less overwhelmed and more prepared to participate in their own care.

“Sometimes the most meaningful support is having someone beside you who is willing to listen carefully, organize the details, and keep looking for the next possible step.”

How I Can Help

Practical support for complicated healthcare moments.

Support is shaped around the patient, caregiver, appointment, and situation. The goal is always greater clarity, preparation, and confidence.

Patient Navigation

Make sense of referrals, specialists, care transitions, follow up needs, and possible next steps.

Medical Research

Locate reputable information about conditions, treatments, rehabilitation, specialists, and care options.

Appointment Preparation

Organize symptoms, history, concerns, goals, and focused questions so important details are easier to discuss.

Information Organization

Create clearer timelines, medication lists, care summaries, question sheets, and records checklists.

Care Coordination Support

Prepare for communication with providers, facilities, caregivers, and services so priorities stay easier to track.

Resource Connection

Look for community, disability, caregiver, transportation, financial, and other practical support resources.

Patient Voice and Goals

Clarify what matters most so you can communicate concerns, preferences, and goals more confidently.

Caregiver Support

Sort through concerns, organize next steps, prepare for conversations, and identify useful resources.

What You Can Expect

Compassion with structure. Support with boundaries.

I want every person I support to feel respected, informed, and in control of their own decisions.

01

Your voice leads.

Your goals, priorities, and comfort level guide the work.

02

Research before assumptions.

I focus on reputable sources and clearly separate information from medical advice.

03

Details matter.

Timelines, records, questions, names, dates, and follow up steps are organized carefully.

04

Persistence stays respectful.

Advocacy can be firm and persistent while remaining clear, professional, and collaborative.

My Approach

Listen. Organize. Prepare. Advocate.

Healthcare can feel chaotic. My role is to help create a clearer path without taking control away from you.

01

Tell me what is happening.

We start with your concerns, priorities, questions, and what feels most overwhelming right now.

02

We organize the situation.

Questions, records, timelines, research, resources, and possible next steps become easier to see.

03

You move forward prepared.

You stay in control of your decisions with clearer information and stronger preparation for the conversation ahead.

Volunteer Advocacy

Currently accepting select volunteer support requests.

I am continuing to build my independent advocacy practice and professional training. I may be able to help with research, organization, appointment preparation, resource navigation, or a complex healthcare situation that needs a clearer plan.

Start With the Intake Form

Volunteer availability is limited. Support is nonclinical and is not medical, legal, or emergency advice.

A Clear Boundary

Advocacy supports your care. It does not replace your care team.

I provide independent, nonclinical support focused on research, organization, preparation, communication, and navigation. I do not diagnose, prescribe, provide treatment, make medical decisions for you, or provide legal advice. Medical decisions should be made with qualified healthcare professionals.
