Before You Begin

Please keep sensitive details out of this form.

Do not include medical records, Social Security numbers, insurance member IDs, account numbers, or other highly sensitive identifying information.

Examples of support

Appointment preparation, medical research, care navigation, information organization, resource finding, caregiver support, and clarifying questions or goals.

Important boundary

Support is nonclinical. I do not diagnose, prescribe, provide treatment, make medical decisions for you, provide legal advice, or offer emergency services.
